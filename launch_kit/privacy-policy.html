Privacy Policy — शायरी (Shayari)

Effective Date: November 2026 • Package ID: com.aistudio.shayari.mxplqr

This Privacy Policy explains how the शायरी (Shayari) Android application collects, uses, and protects your information.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information (Optional Google Sign-In): If you choose to sign in with Google to use Kavi Manch, comments, poet profiles, or cloud sync, we receive your basic Google profile details (display name, user ID, and profile photo URL).
  • User-Created Content: Shayari, poet bios, comments, likes, and follows that you voluntarily publish or save in the app.
  • Local Preferences & Guest Drafts: Reading font preferences, dark/light theme, favorite shayari, and guest drafts are stored locally on your device.

2. How We Use Information

  • To display and sync your personal shayari, poet profile, comments, and community posts via Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore).
  • To provide optional AI writing suggestions when you tap AI Assist in the Write Studio.
  • To deliver optional daily shayari reminders or activity notifications when you enable them.

3. Media & Device Permissions

When you choose a custom background photo for your shayari card, the app uses the privacy-preserving Android Photo Picker and does not request broad access to your device storage. Saving a shayari card image uses Android MediaStore to save only the image you explicitly download.

4. Data Sharing & Security

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. Data stored in Firebase is protected using authenticated access and Firebase security rules. Public Kavi Manch posts and poet profiles are visible to other signed-in community users as intended by the feature.

5. Data Deletion & Control

You can edit or delete your personal shayari, community posts, and comments directly inside the app at any time, or sign out from My Account. To request full account or data deletion, contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store page.

6. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as new features are added. Updates will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
