## 2. Step-by-Step Launch & Google Search Indexing Checklist

1. **Generate AAB from AI Studio:** Open the Settings menu in AI Studio → Generate/Export **AAB (Android App Bundle)**.
2. **Host `launch_kit/` for Free (2 minutes):**
   - Upload the files in `launch_kit/` (`index.html`, `privacy-policy.html`, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`) to **GitHub Pages** or **Firebase Hosting**.
   - Replace `YOUR_DOMAIN` in `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` with your live site URL.
3. **Submit to Google Search Console:**
   - Open https://search.google.com/search-console → Add your hosted site URL → Submit `sitemap.xml` and click **Request Indexing** on your homepage.
4. **Publish on Google Play Console:**
   - Upload your `.aab` file to https://play.google.com/console
   - Paste the **App Name**, **Short Description**, **Full Description** above, and your hosted `privacy-policy.html` URL.
   - Upload 4–6 phone screenshots (Home Feed with Mohabbat/Dard/Zindagi/Dosti/Hausla chips, Reels Reader, Long-Press Menu, Write Studio, Kavi Manch).
5. **Organic Reach & Traffic Playbook (First 14 Days):**
   - Post 2 daily 8-second **Instagram Reels & YouTube Shorts** made directly from your app's Download Card feature with trending instrumental audio + Play Store link in bio/pinned comment.
   - Start a **WhatsApp Channel** ("रोज़ की शायरी") sharing 3 cards daily — every shared text from the app now automatically includes your Play Store download link!
